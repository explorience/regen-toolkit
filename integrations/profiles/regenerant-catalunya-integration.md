# Regenerant Catalunya Integration (first pass)

## What it is
- Regenerant Catalunya: active ReFi BCN program at `03 Libraries/Regenerant-Catalunya`.
- Phase 2 launched (Feb 2026): moving from virtual representation to actual crypto transfers.
- Transfer model: funds to network-level safes (two local partner networks), not individual projects.
- Humm platform for community proposals and voting; manual transfers outside Humm for payments.
- ReFi DAO as co-signers on network safes; networks perform collective funding/distribution role.
- Quartz-based site with program docs, workshops, tools (Safe, Hum/Home Community, IDK), project reports.

## Why it matters for regen-toolkit
- **Active case study pipeline** for Track 2.6 (Funding Mechanisms), 2.7 (Decentralized Governance), 2.9 (Tokenomics), 3.3 (Case Studies by Region).
- Real-world pattern: network-level treasury vs. direct project transfers; legal compliance and receipts.
- Humm as operational tool for community governance and fund management.
- Karma GAP integration for impact documentation; AI-assisted activity report generation.
- Direct content for Mediterranean/Spain regional case studies.
