## How to launch an initiative

1. **Join the network** by signing the covenant and joining ReFi DAO Garden.
2. **Apply as Core Contributor** (required for initiative leads).
3. **Develop and submit your initiative proposal** with deliverables, budget, and metrics.
4. **Activate after approval** with official recognition, channels, and reporting setup.
5. **Operate and report** through regular updates and impact tracking.
