# ReFi BCN Cooperative Formation Playbook

## Executive Summary

This playbook provides a detailed, actionable roadmap for establishing ReFi BCN as a legal **Sociedad Cooperativa Catalana (SCC)** with integrated Web3 governance and regenerative finance focus. The cooperative will serve as a hub for coordinating regenerative projects, managing capital flows via Giveth/ReFi DAO mechanisms, and advancing Catalonia's ecosocial cooperative traditions.

**Key highlights:**
- **Legal entity**: SCC under Catalan Law 12/2015 (democratic, flexible, ICA-aligned)
- **Minimum viable structure**: 2 founding partners + 1 supporting partner (within 5 years)
- **Governance model**: Hybrid council-assembly system with consensus-oriented procedures
- **Tech stack**: GovernanceOS + Giveth/GIVpower + Loomio for decision-making
- **Timeline**: 6–8 months to full operationalization
- **Budget**: €254–€350 (legal registration); €0 tech stack (open-source/free)
- **Success measure**: Functional governance within 3 months; €30k+ capital allocation within 6 months

## 1. Legal Structure: Catalan Cooperative (SCC)

### 1.1 Why Catalan Cooperative?

The **Sociedad Cooperativa Catalana (SCC)** is the optimal legal structure for ReFi BCN because:

1. **Regulatory alignment**: Governed by Law 12/2015 of July 9, which mandates adherence to **International Cooperative Alliance (ICA) principles**—democratic control, voluntary membership, member economic participation—aligning perfectly with regenerative finance values.

2. **Flexibility**: Cooperatives self-regulate through **Articles of Association** (Estatutos Sociales), allowing bespoke governance models while maintaining legal compliance. This enables hybrid Web3 + traditional models.

3. **Tax efficiency**: Spanish cooperatives enjoy favorable tax treatment on cooperative-derived income and surplus distribution to members.

4. **Established precedent**: Catalonia hosts **Catalan Integral Cooperative (CIC)** and **femProcomuns**, demonstrating scalable models for ethical, commons-oriented operations.

5. **Regional support**: Catalonia's government funds cooperative networks and conversion programs, positioning ReFi BCN for institutional backing.

### 1.2 Legal Requirements (Law 12/2015)

[LEGAL REVIEW: Consult licensed Catalan law advisor to confirm statutory requirements and register Articles of Association]

#### Minimum Partners
- **First-degree cooperative** (most-common type): Minimum **2 partners**
- **Consumer/user cooperative** (e.g., ReFi BCN as a funding platform): Minimum **10 natural persons**
- **ReFi BCN recommendation**: Start as **worker/ordinary cooperative** with 2 founders; add 1 supporting/collaborating partner within 5 years to ensure sustainability

#### Minimum Share Capital
- **€3,000 total**, contributed in cash or non-cash (assets, IP, rights with economic value)
- ReFi BCN recommendation: €3,000–€5,000 (low risk, easily met)
- Contributions can be unequal per Articles; examples:
  - Founder 1: €1,500
  - Founder 2: €1,500
  - Supporting partner: €0 (added within 2 years with capital contribution)

#### Required Governing Bodies
- **General Assembly (Asamblea General)**: Sovereign body of all partners
  - Meets ≥1x annually (ordinary); extraordinary as needed
  - One vote per partner (default; weighted voting allowed for ordinary partners if Articles permit)
  - Approves annual accounts, elects Governing Board, modifies statutes
  
- **Governing Board (Consejo Rector)**: Executive body, minimum **3 members**
  - Mostly partners (non-partners capped at 25% max)
  - Sets strategic direction, oversees management, implements Assembly decisions
  - Can have sub-committees for governance, finance, community

#### Legal Name & Registration
- Must include **"sociedad cooperativa catalana" (SCC or SCoopC)** on all documents
- Example: *"ReFi Barcelona, Sociedad Cooperativa Catalana de Regeneración Financiera"*
- Register with **Registro de Cooperativas de Cataluña** (Catalan Cooperative Registry)
- Publish in **BORME** (Boletín Oficial del Registro Mercantil / Commercial Registry Bulletin)

## 2. Governance Model: Hybrid Council-Consensus System

### 2.1 Design Principles

ReFi BCN's governance must balance three forces:

1. **Democratic control** (ICA principle, legal requirement): All partners have voice
2. **Efficiency** (operational reality): Decisions must happen fast for market response
3. **Consensus & commons** (ReFi ethos): Leverage diverse stakeholder input, avoid extractive voting

**Recommended model**: **Tiered Hybrid Governance**

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│      General Assembly (Annual)      │
│   ≥2 meetings/year, binding votes   │
│   Sets strategy, approves accounts  │
└──────────────┬──────────────────────┘
               │
        ┌──────▼────────────────┐
        │  Governing Board      │
        │  (3 members, monthly) │
        │  Strategic decisions  │
        └──────┬────────────────┘
               │
      ┌────────┴────────────────┐
      │                         │
  ┌───▼──────┐        ┌────────▼──┐
  │ Operating│        │ Community │
  │ Committee│        │ Council   │
  │ (Weekly) │        │(Quarterly)│
  └──────────┘        └───────────┘
```

### 2.2 General Assembly (Sovereign Decision Body)

**Frequency**: 
- Ordinary: Annual (statutory, accounts approval)
- Extraordinary: Quarterly (strategic decisions) + ad-hoc (urgent matters)

**Voting**:
- **Default**: One partner = one vote (per Law 12/2015)
- **Quorum**: ≥50% of partners present/proxy
- **Thresholds**:
  - Strategic decisions (new projects, budget >€5k, partnership): Simple majority (>50%)
  - Statute changes, merger/dissolution: 2/3 majority
  - Emergency decisions (e.g., crisis response): Supermajority or consensus

**Consensus option**: For decisions <€5k budget, Assembly may adopt **consensus-based** discussion (Loomio integration—see Section 3.3) before formal vote, reducing friction.

**Attendees**: All partners, advisors (non-voting), community liaisons (observers)

### 2.3 Governing Board (Executive Leadership)

**Composition** (3–5 members, roles):
- **President/Coordinator**: Overall direction, partner representation
- **Secretary/Governance Lead**: Decisions documentation, legal compliance, Assembly organization
- **Treasurer/Finance Officer**: Budget oversight, Giveth/blockchain fund management
- **Community Lead** (optional, if 4+ members): Stakeholder engagement, ReFi network liaison
- **Tech Lead** (optional, if 5 members): Org-os tools, infrastructure, data integrity

**Tenure**: 3-year terms, staggered (ensure continuity), re-eligible once

**Meetings**: 
- Weekly operational sync (30 min, async-friendly)
- Monthly strategic session (2 hours)
- Emergency protocols (48h notice for urgent decisions)

**Authority**:
- Day-to-day operations within approved budget
- Project approvals up to €2,500
- Giveth/GIVpower grant allocation coordination
- Personnel management (if hiring staff)
- Reports to Assembly quarterly

### 2.4 Decision-Making Frameworks

#### A. Routine Decisions (Weekly Ops)
- **Who decides**: Governing Board
- **Tool**: Weekly Slack/Discord + 48h async comment window (Loomio)
- **Threshold**: Consensus or simple majority (3/3 if 3 members)
- **Example**: Content calendar, community event scheduling, minor budget adjustments

#### B. Strategic Decisions (Quarterly/Ad-hoc)
- **Who decides**: General Assembly with Board recommendation
- **Tool**: Loomio + in-person/video Assembly
- **Threshold**: Consensus with veto option (anyone can raise critical concern), escalates to supermajority if consensus fails
- **Example**: New project partnerships, budget allocation ≥€5k, governance model changes

#### C. Urgent Crisis Decisions
- **Who decides**: Governing Board (may convene subset for speed)
- **Tool**: Emergency protocol (24h async + phone call)
- **Threshold**: 2/3 Board majority (Board can act, must inform Assembly within 1 week)
- **Example**: Security breach, regulatory issue, major funder requirement change

## 3. Org-OS Setup Plan: Which Apps, Why, Timeline

### 3.1 What is Org-OS for ReFi BCN?

**Org-OS** (Organizational Operating System) is a metaphorical platform of tools and practices enabling ReFi BCN to:
- Make distributed decisions (voting, consensus, consent)
- Manage resources transparently (budgets, project allocation)
- Archive institutional memory (decisions, meeting notes, policies)
- Scale governance as membership grows

### 3.2 Core Tech Stack

#### **Layer 1: Decision-Making**

**Loomio** (Primary)
- **Why**: Open-source, Catalan-friendly (supports multiple languages), built for consensus
- **Features**:
  - Asynchronous proposals (anyone can propose)
  - Time-bounded polls (48–72h comment window)
  - **Consent-based voting** (can object with reason, escalates to deeper discussion)
  - Decision archive with rationale (institutional memory)
- **Setup**: Free tier supports ≤20 people; upgrade to self-hosted or Community tier (€50–200/mo) if growing
- **Timeline**: Week 2 post-formation
- **Role**: All strategic decisions, Assembly discussions

**GovernanceOS** (Secondary, for Rights Management)
- **Why**: Enables multi-dimensional voting (expertise-weighted, stake-based, role-based)
- **Features**:
  - Custom verification (who has decision-making authority)
  - Weighted voting (e.g., Founding Board vs. Community members)
  - Privacy controls (sensitive budget discussions)
- **Setup**: Open-source, self-hosted on ReFi BCN's server or via managed instance
- **Timeline**: Month 2–3 (after legal formation, once governance rules solidified)
- **Role**: Board-level strategic decisions, weighted by expertise/role

#### **Layer 2: Finance & Fund Coordination**

**Giveth** (Primary blockchain integration)
- **Why**: ReFi-native, direct integration with ReFi DAO and Regenerant Catalunya program
- **Features**:
  - Project pages for ReFi BCN and sub-projects (e.g., specific regenerative initiatives)
  - GIVpower: Lock GIV tokens to boost visibility, vote on fund allocation, earn GIVbacks
  - Milestone-based disbursements (via Trace)
  - Direct donation flow from global ReFi community
- **Setup**: Create ReFi BCN project page (free); integrate wallet (requires crypto setup)
- **Timeline**: Week 4 (post-legal, with crypto-savvy team member)
- **Role**: External fund sourcing, GIV governance participation, ReFi network visibility

**Multi-Sig Wallet** (Gnosis Safe on Optimism or Celo)
- **Why**: Secure fund holding, transparent onchain governance
- **Features**:
  - Multiple signers (3-of-5 typical: President, Treasurer, Community Lead)
  - Transaction logs (auditable, transparent)
  - Gas-efficient (Optimism/Celo chains)
- **Setup**: 30 min, requires MetaMask wallet + €50 fiat for initial gas
- **Timeline**: Week 4, parallel with Giveth setup
- **Role**: Hold EUR-equivalent stablecoins (USDC/cUSD), approve disbursements per Board decisions

**Notion or Obsidian** (Accounting & Docs)
- **Why**: GDPR-compliant, offline-accessible, customizable
- **Features**:
  - Budget tracking, project ledger
  - Policy database (Articles, decisions, risk log)
  - Integration with Loomio decisions (snapshot imports)
- **Setup**: Free tier (Notion) or self-hosted (Obsidian)
- **Timeline**: Week 1 (pre-formation, use immediately)
- **Role**: Institutional memory, audit trail, legal compliance

#### **Layer 3: Community & Communication**

**Discord** (Primary comms hub)
- **Why**: ReFi-native, supports crypto wallet verification, free
- **Features**:
  - Channels for Board, projects, announcements, casual chat
  - Bot integration (tip to call Loomio for voting, reminders)
  - Records (searchable history)
- **Setup**: 1 hour (channels, roles, welcome onboarding)
- **Timeline**: Week 1
- **Role**: Daily coordination, community building, emergencies

**Website/Wiki** (Static + Optional CMS)
- **Why**: Public-facing, SEO, transparency
- **Features**:
  - Overview of ReFi BCN, mission, team
  - Project directory (filterable by category, region, impact)
  - Impact dashboard (live stats on allocated capital, projects supported)
  - Articles of Association (public, translated to EN/ES/CA)
- **Setup**: Static site (free GitHub Pages) or lightweight CMS (Zola, Hugo, or open-source Strapi)
- **Timeline**: Month 2–3
- **Role**: Attracting partners, communicating impact, transparency

## 7. Risk Mitigation Strategies

### 7.1 Legal & Regulatory Risks

#### **Risk**: Statute non-compliance or regulatory drift
- **Mitigation**:
  1. Legal review of Articles annually (Q1 each year)
  2. Secretary tracks regulatory changes (Law 12/2015 amendments, EU cooperative law)
  3. External counsel on retainer (€100–150/quarter advisory)
  4. Insurance policy includes regulatory liability (D&O insurance)

#### **Risk**: Partner disputes over governance or surplus distribution
- **Mitigation**:
  1. **Clear Articles**: Spell out voting, surplus allocation, conflict resolution in Articles
  2. **Mediation protocol**: Engage external mediator before arbitration (cost: €200–500)
  3. **Exit provisions**: Define buyback/withdrawal terms to prevent forced liquidation
  4. **Consensus-culture building**: Use Loomio's consent-based processes to reduce adversarial votes

#### **Risk**: Inability to add 3rd partner within 5 years (legal dissolution threat)
- **Mitigation**:
  1. **Year 2 planning**: Begin recruitment for supporting/collaborating partner by Month 12
  2. **Clear roles**: Define supporting partner role as limited (no Board seat, observer status) to reduce onboarding friction
  3. **Succession planning**: Ensure community members have pathway to formal partnership

#### **Risk**: Tax authority challenge on cooperative classification
- **Mitigation**:
  1. Document cooperative activity clearly (board minutes showing member participation, surplus allocation)
  2. Annual tax filing per cooperative tax code (Tax Agency Form for cooperative societies)
  3. Accounting kept per cooperative accounting standards (Catalan cooperative framework)

---

### 7.2 Financial Risks

#### **Risk**: Mismanagement of blockchain funds (multi-sig wallet)
- **Mitigation**:
  1. **Multi-sig threshold**: 3-of-5 required signatures (President, Treasurer, Community Lead minimum)
  2. **Public ledger**: All transactions logged onchain (Optimism/Celo explorer transparent)
  3. **Spending cap per transaction**: Maximum €5k without Board approval; >€5k requires Assembly vote
  4. **Monthly audit**: Treasurer reports wallet balance, pending transactions to Board
  5. **Cold storage option**: Unused reserve held in hardware wallet, only operational €5k–10k hot

#### **Risk**: Grant clawback (e.g., Regenerant Catalunya funding revoked)
- **Mitigation**:
  1. **Diversified funding sources**: Don't rely solely on Regenerant (target 30% max from any single funder)
  2. **Reserve fund**: Maintain 3-month operating expense buffer (€5k–8k)
  3. **Tracking & reporting**: Document all grant-funded project outcomes meticulously
  4. **Communication**: Monthly touchpoint with funder (Giveth/ReFi DAO governance lead)

#### **Risk**: Crypto volatility (stablecoin depeg, market crash)
- **Mitigation**:
  1. **Stablecoin preference**: Use USDC (Optimism) or cUSD (Celo), not volatile tokens
  2. **Fiat on-ramp**: Convert 50% of grant income to EUR immediately, hold as cash reserve
  3. **GIV exposure limits**: Accept GIV governance tokens only for voting power, liquidate rewards to stablecoins
  4. **Insurance**: Explore crypto-specific insurance for multi-sig wallet (e.g., Nexus Mutual, cost: €200–500/year)

---

### 7.3 Governance Risks

#### **Risk**: Governance paralysis (Assembly can't reach decisions, Loomio stalled)
- **Mitigation**:
  1. **Consent-based voting**: Use Loomio's built-in consent protocol (default threshold: ≥90% consent, else escalate to discussion)
  2. **Timebound decisions**: All proposals must resolve within 14 days max (async vote window)
  3. **Facilitator role**: Assign neutral facilitator (external advisor) if Board-Assembly conflicts arise
  4. **Appeals process**: Decisions reversible by 2/3 Assembly vote within 30 days if material new info emerges

#### **Risk**: Community Council or Board members inactive/non-responsive
- **Mitigation**:
  1. **Role agreements**: Signed commitment to time/availability at onboarding
  2. **Removal process**: Board member absent >2 consecutive meetings can be recalled by Assembly simple majority
  3. **Rotation**: 3-year term limits, staggered so no more than 1 member rotates per year
  4. **Succession pipeline**: Community Council serves as talent pool for Board recruitment

#### **Risk**: Factionalism (sub-groups forming, trust breakdown)
- **Mitigation**:
  1. **Transparent decision-making**: All votes/reasoning logged in Loomio, no backroom deals
  2. **Regular all-hands**: Monthly "State of ReFi BCN" call (all members, async Q&A option)
  3. **Conflict resolution protocol**: Establish neutral mediation process before escalating
  4. **Values alignment**: Quarterly reflection on mission/principles (ICA principles core to Articles)
  5. **Exit grace**: Clear withdrawal terms so unhappy members can leave respectfully

---

### 7.4 Operational Risks

#### **Risk**: Tool failure (Giveth, Loomio, multi-sig wallet compromised)
- **Mitigation**:
  1. **Backup decision systems**: If Loomio down, fall back to Discord poll (lower-stakes) or Notion form (formal)
  2. **Giveth redundancy**: Mirror projects on secondary platform (e.g., Chuffed) if needed
  3. **Multi-sig security**: Private keys stored in 3 separate hardware wallets (Ledger) held by President, Treasurer, Community Lead
  4. **Offline governance**: All decisions ultimately documented in paper/Notion archive (not blockchain-dependent)
  5. **Testing**: Quarterly test of "disaster recovery" (e.g., full Loomio outage sim)

#### **Risk**: Key person dependency (e.g., only Treasurer understands multi-sig)
- **Mitigation**:
  1. **Documentation**: Multi-sig setup documented in Notion (keys stored securely, recovery process clear)
  2. **Cross-training**: Treasurer trains President + one Board member on wallet access/signing quarterly
  3. **Redundancy**: 5-of-5 signers have key access, only 3-of-5 required (increases resilience)
  4. **Succession plan**: Identified backup for each Board role, trained in first 6 months

---

### 7.5 Community & Impact Risks

#### **Risk**: Greenwashing accusations (ReFi BCN claims impact without delivering)
- **Mitigation**:
  1. **Rigorous measurement**: Impact dashboard shows metrics (€ allocated, projects live, outcome data)
  2. **Third-party audit**: Annual impact review by external ReFi researcher (partner with ReFi DAO or academic)
  3. **Transparency**: Publish decision rationale for every project approval
  4. **Community feedback**: Quarterly surveys of supported projects + beneficiaries on actual outcomes

#### **Risk**: Community burnout (too many calls, decisions, volunteer labor)
- **Mitigation**:
  1. **Meeting discipline**: Max 1 mandatory Assembly per quarter; 1 optional Community Council per quarter
  2. **Async-first**: Loomio for decisions, Discord for casual chat, in-person only if critical
  3. **Compensation**: Budget for paying community leads + Project Managers once scaled
  4. **Realistic scope**: Year 1 target: 3–5 active projects only (not 20+)

### B. Template: Articles of Association (Skeleton)

```markdown
# ESTATUTOS SOCIALES
## Sociedad Cooperativa Catalana de Regeneración Financiera "ReFi Barcelona"

### I. DENOMINACIÓN, DOMICILIO, ACTIVIDAD Y NATURALEZA

1.1 Denominación: "ReFi Barcelona, Sociedad Cooperativa Catalana de Regeneración Financiera" (SCC)
1.2 Domicilio social: [Address in Barcelona/Catalonia]
1.3 Actividad principal: Coordinación de financiación regenerativa, gestión de capital para proyectos de sostenibilidad ambiental y social
1.4 Naturaleza: Cooperativa de primer grado, mixta (productores, trabajadores, colaboradores)

### II. SOCIOS, CUOTAS Y APORTACIONES

2.1 Clases de socios:
- Socios ordinarios: Participan en actividad cooperativa y gobierno
- Socios colaboradores: Aportan capital/expertise sin participación activa
- Socios de consumo: Futuros (si expandir a consumidor)

2.2 Número mínimo de socios: 2 ordinarios inicialmente; 3 dentro de 5 años

2.3 Aportación mínima: €3,000 total; distribución según estatutos/acuerdos asamblearios

### III. ÓRGANOS DE LA COOPERATIVA

3.1 Asamblea General: Órgano supremo
- Ordinaria: Anual
- Extraordinaria: Cuando sea necesario
- Quórum: 50% socios presentes o por proxy
- Voto: Un socio = un voto (salvo ponderación aprobada)

3.2 Consejo Rector: Órgano de administración
- Mínimo 3 miembros elegidos por Asamblea
- Mandato: 3 años, estaggered rotation
- Competencias: Dirección estratégica, ejecución de decisiones, reportaje trimestral

3.3 Comisión de Control: Supervisión financiera (opcional, si >30 socios)
- Mínimo 3 miembros
- Revisión semestral de cuentas

### IV. TOMA DE DECISIONES

4.1 Asamblearia: Voto simple mayoría (>50%), salvo:
- Modificación estatutos: 2/3 mayoría
- Disolución: Supermayoría o consenso

4.2 Consejo: Consenso preferido; votación simple si necesario (>50% del Consejo)

4.3 Herramientas digitales: Loomio/Notion para decisiones asincrónicas; consentimiento como base

### V. ECONÓMIA Y DESTINO DE EXCEDENTES

5.1 Ejercicio social: Año natural

5.2 Excedentes: 
- [X]% a reserva legal (reinversión)
- [Y]% a distribución socios (dividendos)
- [Z]% a fondo social/impacto

### VI. RESOLUCIÓN DE CONFLICTOS

6.1 Mediación interna: Facilitador neutral
6.2 Arbitraje: Cámara arbitral si mediación falla
6.3 Recursos: Acciones ante juzgados de lo mercantil si lo anterior agotado

### VII. DISOLUCIÓN Y LIQUIDACIÓN

7.1 Causas: Cumplimiento plazo (si determinado), imposibilidad de objeto, decisión asamblearia
7.2 Reembolso: Contribuciones capitalizadas reembolsadas a socios
7.3 Patrimonio residual: A cooperativas u organismos fines similares (per ICA principles)

```
