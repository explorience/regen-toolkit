# Conflict Resolution for Local Nodes

A practitioner playbook for handling disagreement in a local regen node: most conflict is really about trust/power/being-unheard rather than the surface issue; the Gottman-style escalation ladder (disagreement → contempt → stonewalling); why reaching for a governance mechanism to end a fight tends to break trust instead (the Aragon $200M treasury episode); five NVC-informed principles; a six-step clean conflict process; when tooling like 1Hive's optimistic-governance covenant-challenge pattern or Kleros arbitration genuinely helps; the slow-burn contributor-rewards conflict (SourceCred, Gitcoin stewards); and when to escalate outside the node.
