# How to Get Your First Crypto

A scam-aware, "start tiny" guide for community organizers acquiring their first cryptocurrency, covering three ways in (centralized exchange, peer-to-peer with escrow, earning through paid work/bounties/staking), the energy objection (Bitcoin proof-of-work vs. Ethereum's post-Merge proof-of-stake), and a set of non-negotiable scam-avoidance habits.
