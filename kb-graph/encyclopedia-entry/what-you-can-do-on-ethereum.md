# What You Can Do on Ethereum

A narrowly-scoped beginner guide, written for participants in "the Sarreya programs," that deliberately limits Ethereum's relevance to three concrete uses: (1) sending/receiving digital money (e.g. USDC stablecoin transfers, low "gas" fees), (2) earning verifiable digital credentials for completed trainings/skills, and (3) tracking and proving real-world impact (photo + location submitted and recorded on-chain), which the article names as digital measurement, reporting, and verification (dMRV), citing Silvi as an example program. Explicitly tells readers NOT to worry about NFTs, DeFi lending, staking, or app development.
