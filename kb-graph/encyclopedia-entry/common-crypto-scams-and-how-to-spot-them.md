# Common Crypto Scams and How to Spot Them

A plain-language field guide to the main crypto scam patterns — phishing, investment/"guaranteed returns" fraud, romance/"pig butchering" scams, and rug pulls — plus the psychological levers (urgency, authority, trust) scammers reuse, practical defenses (hardware wallets, 2FA, bookmarked URLs, multi-sig for shared funds), and what to do if you've been scammed (stop contact, document, report to IC3, alert your community).
