# Cooperative Economics and Digital Commons

Explains cooperative economics (worker, consumer, savings-and-loan, and multi-stakeholder cooperatives) grounded in Somaliland/East African traditions (coffee cooperatives, savings circles), extends the idea to the digital commons and DAOs, walks through Elinor Ostrom's eight principles for governing shared resources, and lists funding paths (member contributions, savings-group formalization, grants, quadratic funding, community currencies).
