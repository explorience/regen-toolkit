# Real Examples of Impact DAOs

Introduces Impact DAOs (DAOs organized specifically for positive change) to a permaculture/regenerative-community audience through three named cases — ReFi DAO's global network of place-based Local Nodes, Greenpill's "degens to regens" chapter model with tiered participation, and Klima DAO's approach to bringing verified carbon credits on-chain — and draws an explicit analogy to permaculture principles (working with natural systems, local resilience connected to broader networks, sharing over hoarding, regenerative over extractive economies).
