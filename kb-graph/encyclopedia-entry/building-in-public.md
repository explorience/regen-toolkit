# Building in Public

A short practice guide for local nodes on sharing progress, failures, and learnings openly as they build: posting updates, sharing decisions, and being transparent about challenges. Lists why it works (attracts supporters, creates accountability, builds trust, generates content, helps other nodes), what to share (progress updates, lessons learned, behind-the-scenes decision-making and financial transparency), where to share (Twitter/X, Farcaster, blog, newsletter, Discord/Telegram), and four best practices: be consistent, be honest, be specific, engage with responses.
