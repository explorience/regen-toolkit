# What Is a Knowledge Commons?

A plain-language guide for small teams comfortable with docs/GitHub on building a shared knowledge base that survives team turnover. Distinguishes a "commons" from a plain wiki by governance (drawing on Elinor Ostrom's commons-governance principles and her later "knowledge as a commons" work with Charlotte Hess), argues turnover is the real test of a knowledge base, profiles three real working examples at different scales (Wikipedia, SuperBenefit Knowledge Garden, GravityDAO), introduces the "digital garden" posture (rough/unfinished notes published openly rather than perfected before publishing), and warns against building an ontology before content exists.
