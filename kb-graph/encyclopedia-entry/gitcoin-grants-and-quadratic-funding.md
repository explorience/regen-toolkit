# Gitcoin Grants and Quadratic Funding

How quadratic funding distributes a matching pool by counting contributor count rather than dollar totals (sum-the-square-roots-then-square formula), illustrated with a real regen QF round (GG24's Bioregional Reforestation Round), the sybil-defense mechanisms rounds use (Human Passport proof-of-personhood, Connection-Oriented Cluster Matching), a walkthrough for running your own round on Grants Stack, and burnout guardrails for first-time round organizers.
