# Custodial vs Non-Custodial Wallets: Understanding Who Holds the Keys

Compares custodial wallets (a third party like an exchange holds your private keys, similar to a bank) against non-custodial wallets (you hold your own keys/seed phrase, no intermediary). Walks through the trade-offs of each — recovery, ease of use, DeFi access, privacy, counterparty risk — then gives East-Africa-specific framing (mobile money precedents like M-Pesa, Eva, Telesom ZAAD) and decision guidance for when to choose which.
