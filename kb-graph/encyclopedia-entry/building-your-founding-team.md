# Building Your Founding Team

A guide for organizers standing up a local ReFi node on assembling a small, durable founding team. Argues to start with two or three committed people rather than a crowd, fill roles (convener, builder/doer, treasurer/ops) rather than titles, bring people in through real work rather than abstract membership, and design against burnout from day one (no single point of failure, legitimate rest). Cites named real-world nodes (ReFi Medellín, ReFi Cartagena) and their named founders as worked examples, plus research on founder conflict and DAO burnout.
