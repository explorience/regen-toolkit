# What Web3 Can and Can't Do

An honest capability/limits comparison for community and regenerative work. What Web3 does well, moving value across borders without a gatekeeper, permissionless building, public/hard-to-fake records, and censorship resistance, illustrated with Grassroots Economics' Sarafu network. What it cannot do (yet): cheap high-volume transactions on the main chain (though Layer 2s help), replacing a normal spreadsheet/database, verifying real-world facts (the Toucan/Verra carbon-credit case as the cautionary tale), and keeping activity private by default. Ends with a single screening question ("do multiple parties who don't fully trust each other need to share one record none of them can secretly change?") as the decision rule.
