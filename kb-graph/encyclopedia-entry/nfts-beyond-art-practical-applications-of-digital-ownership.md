# NFTs Beyond Art: Practical Applications of Digital Ownership

Surveys non-collectible uses of NFTs (blockchain records of unique-item ownership): gaming/virtual-economy asset ownership, digital credentials/attestations for informally-trained skills, real-estate fractional ownership, supply-chain/product-provenance tracking, fraud-proof event ticketing with enforced resale rules, and direct-to-fan music releases with automatic royalty splits. Frames the value as permanent, verifiable, hard-to-forge ownership records rather than the speculative art/collectible use case.
