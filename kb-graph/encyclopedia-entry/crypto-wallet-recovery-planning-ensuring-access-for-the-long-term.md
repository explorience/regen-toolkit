# Crypto Wallet Recovery Planning: Ensuring Access for the Long Term

A guide to crypto wallet recovery methods for individuals and regenerative organizations: seed-phrase backups (paper, metal, geographic distribution), social recovery wallets (guardian-based, e.g. Argent), multi-signature wallets (e.g. Safe, 2-of-3 through 4-of-7 configurations), Shamir Secret Sharing, and inheritance/succession planning. Gives context-specific best practices for individuals, organizations, and DAOs, plus common mistakes (single point of failure, improvised secret-splitting, undocumented passphrases, no written procedures, unavailable guardians). Includes region-specific guidance for Somaliland/East Africa (trusted community storage locations where bank deposit boxes are scarce).
