# Is a DAO Right for Your Community?

A plain-language decision guide for permaculture/grassroots communities weighing DAO structure, using a community-garden analogy to explain decentralized/autonomous/organization, listing when a DAO fits (everyone wants a voice, global participation, transparency, rewarding contribution) and when it doesn't (fast emergency decisions, need for legal protection, low tech comfort, governance-attack risk), a five- question discussion guide, and cooperative/sociocracy/simple-voting-tool alternatives.
