# Is Web3 Right for Your Community?

A short decision-framing guide arguing web3 isn't for everyone: it lists when web3 adds value (transparent/verifiable finance, programmable governance, cross-border coordination, connecting to the wider web3 ecosystem, immutable records) versus when it doesn't (small high-trust groups, minimal financial activity, low tech comfort, "a spreadsheet does everything you need"), five questions to ask before adopting it, and recommends a hybrid approach — web3 where it adds clear value, traditional tools elsewhere.
