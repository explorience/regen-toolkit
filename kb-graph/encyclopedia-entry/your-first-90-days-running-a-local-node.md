# Your First 90 Days Running a Local Node

A paced playbook for standing up a ReFi local node without burnout: convene before you build (days 1-30), distribute the work early (days 31-60), and only fund the thing once there's a track record (days 61-90). Draws on ReFi Medellín's founding story (Tereza Bízková) and Commons Social Change Library burnout research, and points to existing networks (ReFi DAO, Greenpill Network, Regen Coordination) so organizers don't have to invent the whole stack.
