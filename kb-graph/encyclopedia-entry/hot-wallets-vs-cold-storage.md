# Hot Wallets vs. Cold Storage

Contrasts internet-connected "hot" wallets (fast, convenient, higher theft risk) with fully offline "cold storage" (hardware or paper, slower to access, much lower theft risk), and recommends a layered custody model — hot for operational funds, hardware cold storage for reserves/grants, multi-signature for organizational decisions.
