# ReFi vs. DeFi vs. TradFi: Three Ways to Move Money

A plain-language comparison of three financial systems for community organizers: TradFi (centralized institutions — banks, credit unions, grant foundations), DeFi (no-gatekeeper smart-contract finance, mostly speculative in practice), and ReFi (DeFi's machinery redirected at ecological/social repair, illustrated with Regen Network's ecocredit registry, Gitcoin's quadratic funding, and Grassroots Economics' Sarafu community currency). Addresses the "doesn't crypto boil the planet" objection via Ethereum's 2022 proof-of-stake switch, and closes with a task-to-tool matching table rather than an either/or framing.
