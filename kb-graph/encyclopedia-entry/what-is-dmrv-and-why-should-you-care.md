# What is dMRV and Why Should You Care?

A beginner-friendly introduction to Digital Measurement, Reporting, and Verification (dMRV) — proving that regenerative work (tree planting, water conservation, grazing-land recovery) really happened, using phones/apps for data collection and either automated (satellite) or human verification. Walks through worked examples from Hargeisa and Berbera, a simple four-step process, and a five-step getting-started guide using tools like KoboToolbox, ODK, or Google Forms.
