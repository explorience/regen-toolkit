# Proposal Writing for DAOs

A short guide to writing DAO governance proposals that pass and get implemented. Lays out a seven-part proposal anatomy (title, summary, background/context, specification, budget, success criteria, risks), recommends informal pre-proposal steps (temperature check, coalition building, preemptively addressing objections), and lists five common mistakes (vague language, no budget, no success criteria, no timeline, surprising the community with an undiscussed proposal).
