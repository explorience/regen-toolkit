# Why Regens Are Interested in Blockchain

An entry-level explainer for permaculturists and community organizers brand new to crypto, framing blockchain as plumbing, not magic, and asking why a "regen" (regenerative practitioner) would care. Names the fears first (scams, energy use, wasting time/looking foolish) and answers each honestly. Covers three concrete draws: funding without a gatekeeper (quadratic funding via Gitcoin), deciding things together in the open (DAOs, Snapshot voting), and ownership that stays local (self-custody, tokens as membership/voting markers, Sarafu as the grounded example). Closes by citing Greenpill Network's "tool-first honesty" stance: start with the community's actual problem, then ask whether blockchain helps.
