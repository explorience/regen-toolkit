# Choosing a Model for Your Local Node

Compares four organizational models local ReFi nodes actually use — the Collective (5-10 people, consensus), Working Groups (10-30, domain autonomy, ReFi DAO's "Network Initiatives" as the network-scale example), the Council (20-50+, consent-based governance, ReFi DAO's Steward Council as the example), and the Cooperative (15+, formal legal entity) — with a size/formality/decision-speed/legal-protection comparison table and guidance to evolve on purpose between models as group size and treasury size grow, rather than in crisis.
