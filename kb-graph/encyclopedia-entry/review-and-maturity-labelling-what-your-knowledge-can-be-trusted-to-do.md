# Review and Maturity: Labelling What Your Knowledge Can Be Trusted To Do

Guidance for labelling how trustworthy each page in a knowledge commons is, so trust survives founder/author turnover. Names the trap ("polished writing is not automatically reviewed knowledge"), borrows three proven maturity ladders (Wikipedia content assessment, ADR/MADR status field, Kubernetes Alpha/Beta/Stable), proposes a shippable four-rung ladder (Draft/Reviewed/Maintained/Deprecated) stored in frontmatter, ties review rigor to risk level, and treats maturity as an ongoing maintenance signal (expiry by date, compost instead of delete) rather than a one-time label.
