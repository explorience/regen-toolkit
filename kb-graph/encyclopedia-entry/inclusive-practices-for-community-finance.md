# Inclusive Practices for Community Finance

Argues that community finance systems default to excluding the same people banks already exclude unless designed otherwise, and lays out five dimensions of inclusion (economic, technical, temporal, social, accessibility), practical strategies (plain language, multiple on-ramps, rotating what gets centered, explicit invitation, accountability), common mistakes (tokenism, one-size-fits-all design, uncompensated burden on marginalized groups), and community-finance-specific barriers (financial literacy, technical barriers, time poverty, trust barriers) with matched solutions.
