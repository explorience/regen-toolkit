# Federation and Portability: Building a Commons That Outlives Its Tools

Argues that a knowledge commons needs both portability (moving your data between tools/people/orgs without rot) and federation (sharing across groups without merging into one database). Recommends a "portability floor" of plain Markdown in Git (readable, diffable, greppable, vendor-independent) with structure kept in frontmatter, then layers federation on top via named public/internal/private boundaries and stable references — from ad hoc shared folders up to protocols like KOI-net (RIDs) or ActivityPub (Mastodon).
