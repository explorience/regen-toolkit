# Treasury Management for DAOs

Practical guide to DAO treasury management: the "native token problem" (thin liquidity, extreme volatility, broken operational continuity when a treasury holds mostly its own governance token), the case for holding 2-3 years of operating expenses in stablecoins, four diversification methods (earning stablecoin revenue, strategic token sales, strategic partnerships, borrowing against tokens), and Maya Protocol as a transitioning-to-DAO-governance example.
