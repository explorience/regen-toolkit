# Writing Grant Proposals

A practical guide for local node builders writing Web3 grant proposals. Distinguishes prospective grants (fund a promise of future work) from retroactive grants (reward already-delivered impact, e.g. Optimism's Retro Funding), explains quadratic funding (QF) math and its implication that "your job isn't only to impress a panel, it's to turn out a lot of real people," lists what a working proposal contains per Gitcoin's own guidance (lead with a TL;DR including how the money will be spent, metrics over promises, show your team, iterate in public), argues that for a local node in a QF round the proposal IS the fundraising campaign (anchored on ReFi DAO's GG18 Local Node round case study), and closes with an explicit warning against single-source grant dependency as a burnout/turnover trap, recommending diversified funding.
