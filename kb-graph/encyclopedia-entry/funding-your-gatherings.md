# Funding Your Gatherings

A four-tier framework for funding local-node gatherings, from zero-budget (shared meals, free venues, skill contributions) through low ($10-100: member contributions, passing the hat, monthly dues), medium ($100-500: local business sponsorship, small grants, web3-native funding via Gitcoin Grants/Giveth/Endaoment) to large ($500+: ticket sales, tiered sponsors, speaker budgets, ecosystem grants like the Ethereum Foundation ESP). Core argument: start with the cheapest thing that works, be transparent about money, and don't outspend your community's actual capacity.
