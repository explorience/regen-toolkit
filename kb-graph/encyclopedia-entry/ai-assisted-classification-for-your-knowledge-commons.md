# AI-Assisted Classification for Your Knowledge Commons

An opinionated guide for small teams on using AI to sort and tag a growing document pile without letting it silently invent its own taxonomy. Distinguishes typing (what kind of thing) from tagging (what it's about), contrasts unsupervised discovery (BERTopic) with supervised bucketing (LLMs against a fixed category list), and argues for running discovery once to design a taxonomy then constraining all later AI output to an enum so the categories stay stable. Closes with a human-in-the-loop discipline: store model confidence, stamp provenance on every tag, and never let an AI suggestion pass as a settled decision.
