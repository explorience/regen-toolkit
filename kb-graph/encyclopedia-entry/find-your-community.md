# Find Your Community

A plain-language guide for community organizers and permaculturists on finding a trustworthy, real-world ReFi (regenerative finance) community without getting scammed or wasting time. Frames ReFi DAO and Greenpill Network as the two established "front doors," recommends listening before committing (field notes, public Telegram, assessment, steward call), and gives concrete rules for telling a real community from a scam (never share a seed phrase, real teams don't DM first, always type the known URL yourself, never send money first, big member counts aren't proof, slow is safe).
