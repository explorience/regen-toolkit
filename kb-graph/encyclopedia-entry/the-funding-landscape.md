# The Funding Landscape

A map of web3 and ReFi-native funding mechanisms for local node builders, organized around one axis: prospective funding (grants, quadratic funding) that pays before the work happens, versus retrospective funding (RetroPGF / Retro Funding, hypercerts) that pays after impact is shown. Also surveys direct grants, streaming/recurring funding, yield-based ("evergreen") funding, and conviction voting, and closes on Sybil/collusion risk in QF and the case for blending funding sources so no single funder sets a node's direction.
