# What Is a DAO?

A plain-language DAO primer for regenerative community organizers, using a "community garden bank account, made visible and rule-bound" analogy. Breaks down the acronym (Decentralized / Autonomous / Organization), contrasts it with a familiar savings circle, names the four core parts of any DAO (treasury, proposals, voting, smart contracts), and walks through the standard tooling (Snapshot for gasless voting, Safe for multi-sig treasury custody, Aragon for no-code DAO creation), plus real regen-space examples (ReFi DAO, Greenpill Network).
