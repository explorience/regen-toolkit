# Which Chain Is Right for Your Project?

A decision-framework guide (Somaliland/East Africa audience, Community- Steward level) for choosing a blockchain among 1,000+ options. Frames the choice around the "blockchain trilemma" (scalability, security, decentralization), maps use cases to chains (Ethereum L1 for high-value DeFi, Layer 2s like Base/Optimism/Arbitrum for general apps, Solana for high-volume consumer apps, Polygon for gaming), gives a TPS/fee comparison table, covers ecosystem/composability, developer-language fit (Solidity vs Rust vs Move), EVM-compatibility, consensus-mechanism energy tradeoffs (PoS vs PoW), and cross-chain bridge risk, closing with a six-question decision framework.
