# Multisig Setup for Community Treasuries

A step-by-step guide to multi-signature wallets for community treasuries: what a multisig is and why it eliminates single points of failure, threshold configurations (2-of-3, 3-of-5, 4-of-7) and how to choose signers (geographic spread, role diversity, technical capacity), timelocks for large transactions, a five-step setup process on Gnosis Safe, ongoing operations (proposing/approving transactions), security best practices, common mistakes, and social recovery (Argent, SoulWallet) as an alternative for individual (not community) wallets.
