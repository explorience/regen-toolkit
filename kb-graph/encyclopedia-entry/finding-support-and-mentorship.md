# Finding Support and Mentorship

A short guide for local-node builders on finding mentors, peer networks, technical support, and funding support. Names four support types (mentorship via other node leaders/cooperative-development orgs/community foundations/social-enterprise mentors; peer networks of similarly-staged nodes; technical support via developer communities and protocol teams; funding support via Gitcoin Grants, Giveth, and local funds), four ways to find mentors, and norms for being a good mentee.
