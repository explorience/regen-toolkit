# Case Studies: How Nonprofits Are Using Web3

Surveys real-world nonprofit adoption of crypto: Ukraine's 2022 emergency crypto fundraising after banks froze, Mercy Corps Ventures cutting delivery costs 60% with stablecoins in Syria, and the fast growth of crypto donations reported by The Giving Block. Frames web3 tools as practical infrastructure (fast settlement, transparency, lower cross-border fees) rather than a speculative novelty, and gives small nonprofits a low-effort on-ramp (accept crypto donations, use stablecoins for cross-border payments, use blockchain records for transparent reporting).
