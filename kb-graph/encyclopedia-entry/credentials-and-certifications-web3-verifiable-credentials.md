# Credentials and Certifications (Web3 verifiable credentials)

Explains web3 credentials as records tied to a wallet address and signed by an issuer's private key, independently verifiable without contacting the issuer — Verifiable Credentials (VCs) and Soulbound Tokens (SBTs); the trust problem they solve especially where institutions are weak or hard to reach at a distance; active issuers (Gitcoin Passport, Karma, Lens, Galxe, Open Badges); use in grant/DAO gating; and practical setup via MetaMask, Ceramic/IDX, and Disco.xyz. High-risk: this whole topic is identity/reputation infrastructure — see paired public-use-boundary.
