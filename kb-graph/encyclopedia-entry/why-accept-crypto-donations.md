# Why Accept Crypto Donations?

A beginner guide for local organizers (Somaliland/East Africa audience) making the case for accepting cryptocurrency donations: the tax advantage to donors (avoiding capital-gains tax by donating appreciated crypto directly), lower cross-border processing costs than bank wires or card fees, access to a younger/values-aligned donor community, quadratic funding as an amplifier for small donations (via Gitcoin Grants), and honest treatment of concerns (price volatility, security, energy footprint). Recommends four donor-facing platforms (The Giving Block, Endaoment, Every.org, Givepact) that convert crypto to fiat automatically so the receiving organization never has to hold volatile crypto.
