# Building Internal Capacity

A guide on developing the skills and systems a community needs to sustain web3 operations independently, without relying on external experts or consultants. Names four capacity areas (technical: wallet management, smart contract interaction, treasury operations, security; governance: facilitation, proposal writing, conflict resolution, decision-making; communication: writing, social media, community management, outreach; financial: bookkeeping, grant writing, budgeting, fundraising) and a five-step process to build it: identify gaps, map existing skills, create learning pathways, practice with low stakes, and document everything.
