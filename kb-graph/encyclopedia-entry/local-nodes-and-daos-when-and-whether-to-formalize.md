# Local Nodes and DAOs: When (and Whether) to Formalize

Distinguishes a local node (a community concept — people in a place, organized around shared purpose) from a DAO (a legal-and-technical form). Argues for "minimum viable coordination" (SuperBenefit's term) rather than building governance machinery pre-emptively. Names the three concrete things formalizing actually gives you (a treasury no one person controls, decisions anyone can verify, roles that survive people leaving), surveys which DAO tools are actually still standing as of mid-2026 (Safe, Snapshot/Snapshot X, Hats Protocol — with Tally and Coordinape flagged as having wound down), and recommends a hybrid model: informal/human for the social layer, onchain only for the parts that need a trust backstop.
