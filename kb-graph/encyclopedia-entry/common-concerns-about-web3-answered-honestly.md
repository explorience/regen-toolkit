# Common Concerns About Web3, Answered Honestly

A myth-by-myth response to the standard newcomer worries about web3 for permaculturists and community organisers: is it a scam, is it only for the rich, is it just gambling, what about the environment, do I need to understand the tech, and what happens when I make mistakes. Distinguishes fraud (a human problem) from the tool, separates the volatile token from the underlying technology, and gives a precise (not hand-wavy) answer on energy use by contrasting proof-of-work and proof-of-stake networks.
