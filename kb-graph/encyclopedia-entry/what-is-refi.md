# What Is ReFi?

A plain-language introduction to Regenerative Finance for community organizers and growers new to crypto: the "money that builds soil instead of mining it" framing, three concrete problem/solution pairs (Silvi for proving impact, Gitcoin quadratic funding for community-driven grants, local tokens for keeping value circulating), the carbon-tokenization example with its Verra greenwashing caution, the proof-of-stake energy-use correction, and honest limitations.
