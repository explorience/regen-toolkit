# Decentralization and Resilience

Frames decentralization as a practical foundation for community survival rather than a technical abstraction, via four mechanisms — redundancy, fault tolerance, adaptive capacity, and anti-fragility — then introduces "cosmo-local coordination" (global standards + local control) and gives regenerative-community applications (local ReFi nodes, decentralized energy/food networks, community savings circles/ROSCAs) plus six principles for building resilient regeneration.
