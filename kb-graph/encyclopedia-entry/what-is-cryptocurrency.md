# What Is Cryptocurrency?

A plain-language introduction to cryptocurrency for community organizers and growers, using a "shared notebook" analogy for blockchain, drawing the coin/token distinction, and grounding the "why should I care" question in four concrete cases (Sarafu Network local currency, Regen Network ecological credits, Glo Dollar's donation-funding stablecoin, and cheap cross-border remittances), before addressing head-on the three standard fears: environmental impact, scams, and hype.
