# Understanding Gas Fees in Cryptocurrency

A quick, plain-language explainer of blockchain gas fees: what gas is and why it exists (pay validators, prevent spam, allocate block space), the EIP-1559 base-fee + tip fee model (base fee auto-adjusts with demand and is burned; priority fee/tip jumps the queue), typical gas costs by transaction type, what drives fees up or down (network demand, time of day, transaction complexity), and five ways to save (time transactions, avoid volatility spikes, use fee estimators, use Layer 2, set max fees).
