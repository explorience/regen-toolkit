# The Ethereum Ecosystem

A grounded, hype-free tour of Ethereum for regenerative communities in East Africa and Somaliland: what Ethereum is (a shared, permissionless compute platform, not just a currency), Layer 2 networks as a cost/speed fix, DeFi (swaps, lending, stablecoins), NFTs as provable digital ownership, and DAOs as a new organizational form. Closes with practical low-risk starter steps (wallet, L2 browsing, block explorer, browsing a DAO's governance).
