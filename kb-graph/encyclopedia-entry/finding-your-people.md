# Finding Your People

A member-attraction framework for local regen node builders: define your ideal member (values, skills, contribution, needs) and your non-ideal member before looking; look in named online spaces (Discord, Twitter/X, Reddit, LinkedIn) and offline spaces (local meetups, co-working spaces, community events, university groups); attract people through content marketing, community presence, events, and one-on-one relationships; and move them through a four-stage funnel (Awareness, Interest, Visit, Join) toward what ultimately attracts people to stay — authenticity, belonging, purpose, and value.
