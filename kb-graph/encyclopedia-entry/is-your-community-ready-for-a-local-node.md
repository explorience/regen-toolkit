# Is Your Community Ready for a Local Node?

A readiness check for organizers standing up a local ReFi node, arguing that the technology is the easy part and the gap between imagined and actual participation is what kills these projects. Uses the nine-stage Community Readiness Model to set "stage 5, preparation" as the honest launch cutoff, lists green/red flags, gives a three-part readiness test (organize one small thing, handle one small conflict, sustain effort three months), and warns against single-founder dependency using the collapse of Ithaca Hours, the Bristol Pound, and the Brixton Pound as cautionary examples, contrasted with the resilient, multi-stakeholder Sarafu Network.
