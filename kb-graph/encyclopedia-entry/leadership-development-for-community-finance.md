# Leadership Development for Community Finance

How to identify, develop, and distribute leadership within a local node so community finance survives the departure of any single person. Names the "leadership paradox" (need coordination, but concentrated leadership recreates hierarchy), the "founder trap," qualities to look for in emerging leaders (initiative, reliability, relational skill, learning orientation, long-term thinking), a five-part development model, three distribution structures (role rotation, nested leadership, federated structure), five common mistakes, and a five-stage pathway from Observer to Elder. Frames leadership in East African / Somaliland contexts as already relational and contextual (clan elders, women's group leaders, mosque committee members) rather than importing a single-founder model.
