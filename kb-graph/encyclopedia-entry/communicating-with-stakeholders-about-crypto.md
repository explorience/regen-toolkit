# Communicating with Stakeholders About Crypto

A guide for nonprofits on talking to board, staff, and donors before/while adopting crypto donations: educate first, prepare the board on legality, tax, risk and asset-management questions, address volatility/security/ complexity concerns openly, communicate the new option across channels once ready, and build reusable templates.
