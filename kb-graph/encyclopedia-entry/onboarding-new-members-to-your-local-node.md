# Onboarding New Members to Your Local Node

A practical playbook for local-node stewards on welcoming and integrating new members without becoming the bottleneck. Reframes onboarding as a "trellis, not a funnel" — a pre-built structure newcomers climb rather than a conversion pipeline. Centers the first-90-days retention window, a one-buddy-per-newcomer system, a repeatable first-week structure, using web3 tooling (POAP, USSD access) as a welcome mat rather than a gate, and graduating members into roles (observe → first contribution → real role) so onboarding becomes self-sustaining and steward burnout is addressed as part of the onboarding design itself.
