# The History of Bitcoin

A chronological explainer of Bitcoin's origins, foundations-track intro article: the pre-Bitcoin digital-cash experiments (Chaum's ecash, Back's hashcash, Dai's b-money, Szabo's bit gold, Finney's RPOW), Satoshi Nakamoto's October 2008 whitepaper solving the double-spend problem without a central authority, the January 2009 genesis block, the 2010 Bitcoin Pizza Day transaction and the August 2010 value-overflow bug and its fix, Bitcoin's 2011-2012 mainstream milestones, and Satoshi's disappearance. Frames Bitcoin as the culmination of 30+ years of attempts to build money that doesn't depend on central authorities.
