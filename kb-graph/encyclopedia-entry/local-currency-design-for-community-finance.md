# Local Currency Design for Community Finance

A practical guide to designing and launching a community currency: what unit to use (time-based, value-based, points-based), what backs it (nothing/fiat, labour, goods, national currency), who issues it (member-issued, system-issued, hybrid), what rules to set (earning, spending, expiry, fees), and technical implementation choices (paper ledgers, shared digital ledger, blockchain). Covers a three-phase launch process (design, pilot, scale), common mistakes, and named case studies (Ithaca Hours, Brixton Pound, Sardex, BerkShares, and East African chamas/ROSCAs as an existing informal precedent to build on).
