# On-Chain Attestations: Your Digital Proof Record

A beginner-level explainer of on-chain attestations as permanent, verifiable, tamper-proof digital proof records for skills, training completion, volunteering, and community impact — framed as a replacement for paper certificates that can be lost, damaged, or forged. Explains the mechanism in three steps (someone vouches for you → it's recorded permanently on a blockchain → anyone can verify it) and lists use cases: proving skills for scholarships/jobs, building a portable reputation, showing community impact, and accessing opportunity programs that require verifiable proof.
