# DAOs vs Traditional Organizations: A New Way to Coordinate

Contrasts DAOs with traditional hierarchical firms across four dimensions — how decisions get made, organizational structure (flat vs pyramid), and how capital is raised (token issuance vs venture capital/loans/IPOs) — then lists benefits (reduced overhead, transparency, global access, aligned incentives) and honest challenges (voter fatigue, token concentration, legal uncertainty), with East-Africa-specific framing around cooperatives and cross-border participation.
