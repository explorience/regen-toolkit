# Types of Gatherings for Local Nodes

A practical guide for regen local-node organizers to a repertoire of gathering formats — informal (community meal, walk-and-talk, open house), educational (workshop, learning circle, demonstration day), decision-making (town hall using consent-based decision-making, retrospective), and celebration (milestone party, open celebration) — matched to a node's stage of development (pre-formation through renewal), with a sustainable weekly/monthly/quarterly rhythm and named real-world examples from ReFi Cape Town, ReFi Barcelona, and ReFi DAO's global cadence.
