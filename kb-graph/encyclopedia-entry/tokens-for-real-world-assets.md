# Tokens for Real-World Assets

Introduces real-world-asset (RWA) tokenization: turning off-chain valuable things (real estate, gold, bonds, art, carbon credits) into blockchain tokens that prove ownership and can be fractionally traded. Covers the claimed benefits (liquidity, fractional access, transparency, lower cost, global access), the real challenges (legal complexity across borders, valuation/oracle dependence, custody of the physical asset, scalability), the actors involved (issuers, platforms, liquidity providers, service providers), and beginner action items.
