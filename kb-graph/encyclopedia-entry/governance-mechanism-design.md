# Governance Mechanism Design

A framework for designing DAO/community governance mechanisms: six core mechanisms (token-weighted voting, one-person-one-vote, quadratic voting, conviction voting, consent-based decision making, futarchy) each with a best-fit context and named risks; a decision-type-to-mechanism mapping table; five design principles (match mechanism to context, minimize complexity, build in safeguards, iterate, transparency); and anti-manipulation measures against Sybil attacks, whale dominance, and collusion. Recommends starting with consent-based decisions for small groups and adding quadratic voting plus delegation as the group grows.
