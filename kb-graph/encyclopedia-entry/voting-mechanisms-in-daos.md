# Voting Mechanisms in DAOs

Compares six DAO decision-making mechanisms — token-based quorum voting, quadratic voting, conviction voting, holographic consensus, multi-sig voting, and off-chain voting via Snapshot — on the trade-offs of speed vs inclusivity, simplicity vs manipulation-resistance, and participation vs scalability, and notes that most mature DAOs combine several mechanisms rather than relying on one.
