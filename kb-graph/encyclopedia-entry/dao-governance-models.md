# DAO Governance Models

Surveys five DAO governance models — token-weighted voting, quadratic voting, reputation-based governance, multi-signature governance, and hybrid governance — with pros/cons and a named example DAO for each, then gives guidance on choosing a model based on purpose, size, desired decentralization, speed-vs-fairness, and scalability.
