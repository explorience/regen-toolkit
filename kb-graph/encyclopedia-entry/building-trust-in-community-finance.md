# Building Trust in Community Finance

A guide to building and protecting the trust a local node's community finance (timebank, local currency, shared treasury) runs on, for organizers already working in regen/web3. Distinguishes verification (proof of what happened) from trust (belief people will do what they say), names three kinds of trust to build deliberately (competence, integrity, benevolence), lists concrete trust-building practices (transparency by default, consistency, owning mistakes, keeping commitments, inclusion, celebrating trustworthy behavior), addresses the "trust velocity" bootstrapping paradox, names early warning signs of trust breakdown, and gives a five-step repair process after a breach. Cites Fukuyama's social-trust thesis and Grassroots Economics' Sarafu Network as worked evidence.
