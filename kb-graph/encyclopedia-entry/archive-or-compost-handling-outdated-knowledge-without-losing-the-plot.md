# Archive or Compost: Handling Outdated Knowledge Without Losing the Plot

A practical framework for retiring stale documents in a knowledge commons without deleting institutional memory or confusing readers. Names four moves — archive (preserve as labeled history), compost (harvest the still -good parts into living pages, retire the husk), deprecate (mark obsolete with a warning and a pointer), and remove (rare; last resort) — against the Regen Knowledge Commons Toolkit's Evolution Layer and cross-cutting principle #17, "compost, archive, and memory." Closes on the governance rule that keeps this from becoming chaos: a staleness flag is a signal, not a conclusion — a named steward decides which move applies and does it on the record.
