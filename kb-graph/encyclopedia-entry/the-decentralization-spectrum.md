# The Decentralization Spectrum

Frames decentralization as a spectrum rather than a binary. Covers three network-architecture types (centralized, decentralized, distributed), four governance forms (deconcentration, delegation, devolution, privatization), and two cross-cutting dimensions (technological vs. sociopolitical decentralization) that must both be evaluated to judge how decentralized a system really is. Argues decentralization is a design tool to be tuned to context, not an ideology to maximize.
