# Centralized vs. Decentralized Systems: What It Means for Your Garden and Community

A foundations-track explainer contrasting centralized systems (one authority in charge — banks, concentrated food-processing, single- telecom mobile money) against decentralized systems (control spread across many independent parts — cooperatives, rotating savings circles, community seed banks, Bitcoin), aimed at community gardeners and permaculturists new to crypto. Argues most real systems are hybrids on a spectrum, ties the distinction to permaculture's resilience-through- diversity principle, and cites a 2022 food-systems resilience study as supporting evidence while noting the same study found centralization can help during labor shortages.
