# Ontology vs Taxonomy: Which One Does Your Commons Need?

A plain-language guide for teams building a knowledge commons: a taxonomy is a labelled filing cabinet (hierarchy of categories, preferred/alternate labels, broader/narrower/related relations) for browsing and finding things; an ontology adds typed classes, individuals, and named relationships that let software infer facts (e.g. flagging a missing report). The practical heuristic: taxonomy first, ontology only when a specific, recurring task needs machine reasoning over relationships. SKOS is offered as the bridge that keeps the door to an ontology open without wasted work.
