# Human Passport

Formerly Gitcoin Passport — a proof-of-personhood / sybil-resistance tool letting a node confirm members are unique real people, not one person wearing ten wallets. Article frames it as scaffolding toward human trust for larger, open networks, not a permanent replacement for it.
