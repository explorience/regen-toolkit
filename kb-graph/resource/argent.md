# Argent

Mobile-focused wallet with social recovery built in (a trusted guardian can help recover access instead of requiring a multisig threshold). Article frames social recovery and multisig as addressing different problems: simpler/more flexible for individual wallets, but multisig "remains the better choice" for community treasuries needing ongoing, legitimate multi-person access.
