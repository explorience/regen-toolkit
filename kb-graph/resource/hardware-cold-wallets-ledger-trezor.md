# Hardware / cold wallets (Ledger, Trezor)

Small offline devices (about the size of a USB stick) that hold keys off the internet and only approve a payment when physically confirmed on the device; article's example of cold storage for larger holdings. Grouped as one entry (named examples Ledger and Trezor) rather than two, since the article treats them as one category with no distinguishing detail between the two brands.
