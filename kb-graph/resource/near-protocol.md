# Near Protocol

Prioritizes developer experience and user accessibility; uses "Nightshade" sharding to process blockchain data in parallel pieces, and leans on account abstraction to make wallet experiences feel more familiar to non-crypto-native users. Article notes Near has funded carbon removal for its own operations and frames its usability focus as aligned with bringing web3 to people outside the space already.
