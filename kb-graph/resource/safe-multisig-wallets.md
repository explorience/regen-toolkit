# Safe (multisig wallets)

Standard multisig wallet (formerly Gnosis Safe) for on-chain treasuries. Article recommends a 3-of-5 signer setup as a transparency practice: every member gets a public, real-time ledger of every movement, and no single person can move funds alone.
