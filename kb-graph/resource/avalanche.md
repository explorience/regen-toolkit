# Avalanche

Uses a consensus mechanism distinct from Proof of Work and standard Proof of Stake to process thousands of transactions per second while maintaining decentralization; its subnet architecture lets a project spin up an independent blockchain network with its own tokens, rules, and validator set within the Avalanche ecosystem. Article suggests subnets fit a carbon-tracking network, community currency, or local regenerative economy that wants its own chain while staying connected to a larger ecosystem.
