# Otter.ai

Meeting transcription tool, named as the default place a small team should start. Free tier: 300 transcription minutes/month, capped at 30 minutes per conversation, with automated summaries and action items. Pro plan ($8.33/month billed annually) lifts to 1,200 minutes. Article warns transcription pricing changes often and to verify current limits before committing.
