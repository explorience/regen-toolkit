# Cosmos

Lets a project launch its own purpose-built blockchain (via the Cosmos SDK and Tendermint consensus) that stays interoperable with other chains via the Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC) protocol. Article frames this as suited to projects needing full control over their own economics (e.g. a regional community currency with tailored governance) but flags running your own chain — validators, community participation, maintenance — as a major commitment, "probably overkill" for most regenerative communities.
