# Solana

High-throughput (tens of thousands TPS), sub-cent-fee chain using Proof of History to parallelize transaction processing. Article flags network outage history and non-EVM tooling/skills as tradeoffs. Suited, per the article, to high-frequency low-value transactions: community token distributions, micro-donations, at-scale carbon-credit tracking.
