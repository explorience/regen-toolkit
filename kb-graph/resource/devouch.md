# DeVouch

"A system for members of reputable organizations in the Ethereum ecosystem to vouch for or flag projects that are looking to raise funding" (quoted in the article). Decentralizes Giveth's project verification: trusted verifiers issue on-chain vouches worth 1, 3, or 30 points by tier; 3 points unlocks GIVpower boost, 30 points unlocks donor GIVbacks eligibility.
