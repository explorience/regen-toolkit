# Ethereum Attestation Service (EAS)

Open standard for recording signed, tamper-evident attestations (on-chain or off-chain, free schemas); article states over 9.5 million attestations recorded as of mid-2026. Positioned as the recommended plumbing for a local node to attest workshops, plantings, or shipped deliverables. Explorer: easscan.org.
