# SKOS — Simple Knowledge Organization System (W3C)

W3C standard (published 18 August 2009) for representing taxonomies, thesauri, and controlled vocabularies as linked data — prefLabel, altLabel, broader, narrower, related. Cited in ontology-vs-taxonomy.md as the recommended bridge: build a taxonomy in SKOS now, keep the door open to RDF/RDFS/OWL ontology work later without redoing it.
