# Toucan Protocol

Infrastructure that turns verified carbon credits into digital tokens; per the article, accounts for roughly 85% of digital carbon-credit volume and has supported ~$4B in trading volume, working alongside Gold Standard and Puro.earth. Involved in a 2023 Verra policy dispute over tokenizing already-retired credits (see linked claim-evidence).
