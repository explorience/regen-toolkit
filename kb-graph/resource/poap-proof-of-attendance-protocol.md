# POAP (Proof of Attendance Protocol)

Cited in the article as a way to make a newcomer's first event a "small gift, not a test" — a free collectible NFT minted to attendees, issued on Gnosis Chain so both organizers and attendees pay nothing. Distributed via QR code at the event.
