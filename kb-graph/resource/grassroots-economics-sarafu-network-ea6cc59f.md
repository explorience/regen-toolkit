# Grassroots Economics: Sarafu Network

A Kenya-based network letting people exchange Community Asset Vouchers (promises of future goods/labor/services) through shared "commitment pools" that track who has offered, taken, and fulfilled promises over time. Cited in the article as the clearest working example of trust backed by relationships rather than substituted by technology.
