# Hats Protocol

Turns each responsibility (treasury signer, onboarding lead, events coordinator) into an onchain "hat" (ERC-1155 token) so permissions follow the role rather than the person; article states it is trusted by 50+ DAOs. Recommended only once "who is allowed to do what" is a real source of confusion, not pre-emptively.
