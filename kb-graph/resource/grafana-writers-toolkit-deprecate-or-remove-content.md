# Grafana Writers' Toolkit — deprecate or remove content

Draws a sharp line: deprecated means "planned for removal in a future release," removed means "removed and no longer supported." Grafana notifies users two minor releases in advance before anything disappears, with a stock notice template ("Starting with [release], [feature] is deprecated. It will be removed in a future release.").
