# Capital Gains (Crypto, US)

Under US tax treatment, crypto is property; capital gains/losses are calculated from cost basis (what you paid, including fees), and taxed differently depending on holding period: short-term (held ≤1 year, taxed as ordinary income) vs long-term (held >1 year, taxed at 0/15/20%).
