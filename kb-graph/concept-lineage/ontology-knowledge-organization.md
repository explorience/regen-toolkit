# Ontology (knowledge organization)

Describes the kinds of things (classes) in a domain and the specific, named, typed relationships between them and their instances (individuals), in a form a computer can reason over — enabling inference (e.g. flagging a Grant missing a required Report) that a taxonomy cannot do.
