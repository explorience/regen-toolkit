# No single point of failure (organizational crypto custody)

"The most critical rule for organizational crypto security: no single individual should have full control over the organization's funds." Operationalized via multi-sig wallets (multiple keys required to authorize any transaction) and layered role-based access control (view-only operations staff, capped-limit managers, approval-authority executives, unlimited-approval board), plus a hot/cold wallet split so a compromised operational wallet never exposes treasury reserves.
