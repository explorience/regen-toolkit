# Anatomy of a good DAO proposal

A seven-part structure for a governance proposal: (1) a clear, specific title, (2) a 2-3 sentence summary of what's proposed/costs/outcome, (3) background/context explaining the need and evidence, (4) a specification of exact deliverables/timeline/responsible parties, (5) a detailed budget breakdown, (6) measurable success criteria, and (7) a risks section with mitigations.
