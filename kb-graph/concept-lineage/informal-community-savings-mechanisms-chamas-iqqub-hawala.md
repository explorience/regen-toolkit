# Informal community savings mechanisms (chamas, iqqub, hawala)

Community-run informal savings and value-transfer systems named in inclusive-practices.md as long-standing alternatives to formal banking in East Africa and the Horn of Africa — Kenyan chamas (savings/investment groups), Ethiopian/Eritrean iqqub (rotating savings associations), and hawala (informal value-transfer networks).
