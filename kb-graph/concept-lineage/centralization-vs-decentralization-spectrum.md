# Centralization vs decentralization spectrum

Centralized systems put one authority in charge of a resource or decision (a bank, a single telecom's mobile-money network, a handful of large farms); decentralized systems spread control across many independent parts that collaborate toward shared goals (cooperatives, rotating savings circles, seed banks, Bitcoin). Most real systems are hybrids somewhere on a spectrum between the two poles.
