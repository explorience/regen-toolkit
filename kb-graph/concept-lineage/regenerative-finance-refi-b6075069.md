# Regenerative Finance (ReFi)

Takes DeFi's smart-contract machinery (no-gatekeeper lending, funding, exchange) and points it at ecological and social repair rather than pure financial return. The article's framing: DeFi asks "how do we make money?"; ReFi asks "how do we use money to heal soil, water, forests, and communities?"
