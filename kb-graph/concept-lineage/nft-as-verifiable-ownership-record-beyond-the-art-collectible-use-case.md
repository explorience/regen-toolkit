# NFT as verifiable ownership record (beyond the art/collectible use case)

An NFT (non-fungible token) is a blockchain record asserting unique-item ownership. The article's core reframe: the underlying capability isn't "digital art" — it's permanent, tamper-resistant, instantly-verifiable proof of ownership or credential, which generalizes to gaming assets, credentials, property, supply-chain provenance, tickets, and music rights.
