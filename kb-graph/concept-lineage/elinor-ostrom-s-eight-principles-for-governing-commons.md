# Elinor Ostrom's Eight Principles for Governing Commons

Eight design principles (clear boundaries, locally-fit rules, participatory decision-making, self-monitoring, graduated sanctions, conflict-resolution mechanisms, recognized right to organize, nested governance) that Nobel-winning research found in communities that successfully self-govern shared resources without privatization or top-down state control.
