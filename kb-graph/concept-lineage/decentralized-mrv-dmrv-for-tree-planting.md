# Decentralized MRV (dMRV) for Tree Planting

Using a smartphone (photo + geolocation), AI verification, community review, and blockchain recording to monitor, report, and verify individual tree-planting actions, replacing traditional institution-mediated MRV with a phone-and-protocol pipeline.
