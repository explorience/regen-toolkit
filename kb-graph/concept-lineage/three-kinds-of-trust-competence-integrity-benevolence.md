# Three kinds of trust: competence, integrity, benevolence

A three-part model for the trust a community finance system needs: competence trust ("can you do what you say?"), integrity trust ("will you do what you said?", built through consistency), and benevolence trust ("do you care about me?", built by listening and helping without keeping score).
