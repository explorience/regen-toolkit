# Community momentum flywheel and rhythm model

A self-reinforcing cycle (ship → celebrate → invite → onboard → empower → contribute → acknowledge → repeat) paired with predictable rhythms at weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual cadences, that keeps a local node's energy from fading after its initial launch excitement.
