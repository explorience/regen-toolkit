# Archive / Compost / Deprecate / Remove (the four moves)

The four ways a knowledge commons can handle a document that has stopped being true: archive (preserve as a labeled historical record), compost (harvest the still-good parts into living pages, then retire the husk), deprecate (mark obsolete with a warning banner pointing to a replacement), and remove (delete outright — treated as rare).
