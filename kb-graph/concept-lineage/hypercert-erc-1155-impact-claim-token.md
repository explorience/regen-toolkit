# Hypercert (ERC-1155 impact-claim token)

A digital token, built on the ERC-1155 multi-token standard on Ethereum, that captures who did what work, where, and during what timeframe, sold prospectively (before work, to raise capital) or retrospectively (after impact, to claim funding for completed work), and that can be split or merged so funders can support exactly the slice of impact they care about.
