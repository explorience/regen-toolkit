# Stewardship Roles (Knowledge Commons)

Five named jobs — Editor, Curator, Facilitator, Maintainer, Onboarder — that together cover the tending a knowledge commons needs to survive turnover: publish-worthiness, routing/composting, conflict-hosting, upkeep, and onboarding legibility.
