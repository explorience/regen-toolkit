# Purpose-People-Place-Process-Practice (5P gathering framework)

A five-element design framework for gatherings — a specific purpose, a deliberate guest list, a matched space, a designed process arc (opening/ body/closing), and post-gathering practice (follow-through) — treating every gathering as an intentional design challenge rather than a default meetup script.
