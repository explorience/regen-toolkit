# Node founding-team roles: convener, builder/doer, treasurer-ops

A minimal role model for a local node's founding team, filling jobs rather than titles — the convener (runs meetings, holds the calendar), the builder/doer (ships events/workshops/on-chain actions), and the treasurer/ops (holds the multisig, tracks grants, keeps the spreadsheet).
