# Peer-to-peer (P2P) crypto exchange with escrow

A way to buy crypto directly from another person rather than a company: the platform locks the seller's crypto in escrow, the buyer pays the seller directly (bank transfer, PayPal, etc.), and escrow releases the crypto only after the buyer confirms payment.
