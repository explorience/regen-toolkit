# On-chain attestation as a vouching record

A digital certificate/proof-record model with three steps: (1) a trusted party — teacher, mentor, organization, or system — "vouches for you" by making a statement, (2) the statement is written to a blockchain where it "cannot be changed, deleted, or faked," (3) anyone can verify it without contacting the original issuer. The article frames this as "trust without needing a middleman."
