# ERC-721 (Non-Fungible Token Standard)

An Ethereum token standard, launched in 2018, in which every token is unique and individually identified within its contract — used for digital art/collectibles, event tickets, ownership certificates, and impact tokens like carbon-removal or biodiversity credits.
