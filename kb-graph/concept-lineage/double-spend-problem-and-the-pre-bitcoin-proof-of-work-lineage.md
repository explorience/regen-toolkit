# Double-spend problem and the pre-Bitcoin proof-of-work lineage

The problem of preventing someone from spending the same unit of digital money twice without a trusted central bank to check balances — the central puzzle Bitcoin's 2008 whitepaper solved by having every participant keep a shared, proof-of-work-verified record of all transactions.
