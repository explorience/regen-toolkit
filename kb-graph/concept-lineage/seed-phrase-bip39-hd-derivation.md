# Seed Phrase (BIP39 / HD Derivation)

A 12- or 24-word mnemonic, generated per the BIP39 standard from a random number and a fixed 2,048-word list, that acts as the master backup for a wallet's private keys and addresses across every BIP39-compatible chain and wallet app.
