# Regenerative Finance (ReFi)

Money flows that "build soil instead of mining it" — the article's core framing: rather than being extractive (treating a forest as worth more cut down than alive) or merely sustainable (doing less harm), ReFi aims to leave the system measurably healthier than it found it, using blockchain as a shared, tamper-resistant public record.
