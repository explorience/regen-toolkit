# Outputs vs. Outcomes vs. Impact

The core distinction the impact-measurement field rests on: outputs are countable activity ("we ran 12 workshops"), outcomes are what actually changed for people or ecosystems as a result ("30 farmers adopted a regenerative practice"), and impact is the difference the work made versus what would have happened anyway (if the trees would have been planted regardless, impact is zero even with 400 outputs).
