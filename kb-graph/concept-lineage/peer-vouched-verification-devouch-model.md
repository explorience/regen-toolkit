# Peer-Vouched Verification (DeVouch Model)

A decentralized alternative to a single-authority "verified" stamp: trusted verifiers across the ecosystem issue on-chain vouches (worth 1, 3, or 30 points by tier) for a project, replacing one central office's approval decision.
