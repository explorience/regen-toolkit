# Proof of work vs. proof of stake

Two blockchain consensus mechanisms for agreeing what's true across nodes without a central authority. Proof of work secures the chain by burning computing power/electricity (Bitcoin); proof of stake secures it through deposited funds rather than raw computation (Ethereum since its 2022 "Merge," and Celo).
