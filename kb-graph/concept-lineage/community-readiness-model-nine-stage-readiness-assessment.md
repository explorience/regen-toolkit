# Community Readiness Model (nine-stage readiness assessment)

A nine-stage framework (from "no awareness" through "preplanning," "preparation," "initiation," to "community ownership") for assessing how ready a community actually is to take on a new initiative, developed for public health work and applied here to launching a local ReFi node.
