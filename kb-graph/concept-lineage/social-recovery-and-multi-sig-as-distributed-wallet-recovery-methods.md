# Social recovery and multi-sig as distributed wallet-recovery methods

Two distinct ways to remove a single point of failure from crypto wallet access. Social recovery designates 3-5 trusted "guardians" who can collectively help restore access to a lost wallet (typically with a threshold approval and a time-lock window for the owner to cancel unauthorized attempts). Multi-signature (multi-sig) requires multiple private keys to authorize any transaction in the first place (e.g. 2-of-3, 3-of-5, 4-of-7), functioning as both ongoing security and de facto recovery redundancy.
