# DAO Voting Mechanisms

The family of mechanisms DAOs use to turn proposals into decisions — ranging from simple token-weighted quorum voting to quadratic voting, time-weighted conviction voting, prediction-market-based holographic consensus, small-group multi-sig approval, and gasless off-chain signature voting.
