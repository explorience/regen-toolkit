# One job per event (educational / social / working / celebration)

A design constraint for community events: give each gathering exactly one of four jobs — educational (teach one thing), social (build relationships, no agenda), working (make concrete progress together), or celebration (mark a win, thank contributors) — rather than trying to do all of them in one evening.
