# Impact DAO

A DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization) whose shared mission is specifically to create positive change — restoring ecosystems, funding local projects, or building alternative economic systems — rather than a DAO organized around, e.g., a protocol, an investment fund, or a social club.
