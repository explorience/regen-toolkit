# Crypto Wallet (keys, not money)

A crypto wallet does not store cryptocurrency itself — funds live on the blockchain. A wallet holds the cryptographic keys (a public key/address to receive with, and a private key that must never be shared) that prove a slice of the shared ledger belongs to you.
