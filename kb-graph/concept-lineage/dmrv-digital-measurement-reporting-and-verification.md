# dMRV (Digital Measurement, Reporting, and Verification)

A system for turning real-world actions (e.g. planting a tree) into unchangeable, verifiable proof by recording photos, location, and other data on the blockchain — used to attract funding, demonstrate accountability, and prove contributions.
