# Evergreen notes / seedling-budding-evergreen maturity taxonomy

A practice of writing notes that "evolve, contribute, and accumulate over time, across projects" (Andy Matuschak's definition), paired with a visible maturity label — seedling, budding, evergreen — so a reader can tell at a glance whether they're looking at a rough sketch or a settled idea (Maggie Appleton's digital-garden taxonomy).
