# Digital Measurement, Reporting, and Verification (dMRV)

Using phones/computers to measure what happened (e.g. trees planted), report that information, and verify it is true — the "digital" version of traditional MRV (Measurement, Reporting, Verification) used in environmental/carbon programs.
