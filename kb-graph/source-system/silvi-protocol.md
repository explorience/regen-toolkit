# Silvi Protocol

A mobile-app-based protocol for documenting tree planting: photo + geolocation capture, AI + community verification of tree existence and species, and automated stablecoin (cUSD/USDC) payment to planters once verified, all recorded on-chain. Integrates the Treekipedia tree-species database.
