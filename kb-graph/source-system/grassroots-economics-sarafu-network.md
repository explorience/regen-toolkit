# Grassroots Economics (Sarafu Network)

Sarafu, a community currency over 50,000 people have used to trade food, goods, and services where regular cash/banking is scarce. Works on basic phones over USSD; the transaction record lives on a public blockchain so the community, not a bank, governs it.
